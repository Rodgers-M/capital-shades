# Project Decisions

## 2026-10 — Terminology

Decision:
Use "Solutions" rather than "Products".

Reason:
Capital Shades delivers designed/fabricated/installed
structures rather than simply selling packaged products.

## 2026-10 — Sectors

Decision:
Residential, Commercial and Institutional are cross-cutting
content dimensions, not standalone duplicated content trees.

Reason:
Prevents duplicate content and connects naturally to projects.

## 2026-10 — Mobile conversion

Decision:
Use Call / WhatsApp / Request Quote action bar.

Behavior:
Appear after hero and hide during form interaction.

## Public settings exposure

Decision:
Client-facing loaders must return only public business settings rather
than serializing the complete CMS/settings record.

Reason:
The CMS may contain secondary or internal contact information that should
not automatically become visible in browser page data.

Public contact details must use the explicitly selected primary values.

## Claims and technical wording

Until confirmed by the owner or supported by explicit source data:

- avoid absolute claims such as "every", "100%", "all", "any size"
- avoid subjective quality claims such as "best", "high quality",
  "good value", "professionally..."
- avoid operational claims such as "our team", "in-house", or similar
- prefer descriptive, observable wording

## Geographic positioning

Capital Shades primarily operates in Nairobi and nearby areas that are
practical to serve regularly.

Primary market context includes:

- Nairobi
- Kiambu County
- Machakos County
- Kajiado County

Projects elsewhere in Kenya may also be considered.

The website should therefore present Capital Shades as Nairobi-centered,
not Nairobi-exclusive.

Do not make broad claims such as:

- "serving all of Kenya"
- "available nationwide"
- "projects across Kenya"

unless confirmed by the owner.

Do not remove useful Nairobi/location context where it is relevant and
accurate.

## Local SEO direction

A dedicated SEO/local-search pass will be done later.

The content architecture should make it possible to target searches such as:

- car park shades Nairobi
- car shade solutions Nairobi
- shade structures Nairobi
- car park shades Kasarani
- shade solutions Thika Road
- car park shades Kiambu
- shade structures Machakos
- shade solutions Kajiado

Do not keyword-stuff current page copy.

For now:

- keep solution pages focused on the service itself;
- preserve structured location/service-area fields in the content model;
- keep project location support;
- use verified geographic references naturally when available;
- defer dedicated local landing pages and SEO copy to the SEO phase.
