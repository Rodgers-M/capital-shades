# 5. Project process

Confirmed typical process:

1. Customer enquiry
2. Initial discussion
3. Site visit
4. Measurements
5. Design or proposal
6. Quotation
7. Deposit or approval
8. Fabrication
9. Installation
10. Handover
11. After-sales support

## Site visit

A site visit is required.

Site visits may be free within Nairobi and nearby areas such as Kiambu,
depending on how far the site is from Nairobi.

Do not publish a blanket "free site visits everywhere" statement.

## Measurements

Capital Shades performs the project measurements.

## Design

Drawings, renders or technical designs can be prepared upon customer request.

## Fabrication

Fabrication may take place:

- at the customer's site; or
- at a Capital Shades workshop.

This may depend on the project and customer preference.

## Quoting

The business normally works quickly on quotations.

For website expectations, use the safer distinction:

- details/photos may support an initial estimate;
- final quotation follows the site visit and confirmed measurements.
